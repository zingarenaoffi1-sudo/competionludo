Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Zingarena Ludo ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Zing Arena. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("players", "you").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you download, install, access, or play our mobile application and related online services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Google Sign-In Profile: When you sign in via Google (Firebase Authentication), we access public basic profile data such as your display name, email address, profile photo, and unique Firebase user ID (UID). We do NOT have access to or store your Google account password.
  • Guest Profile Data: If you play as a guest or pass-and-play offline, we generate a local pseudonymous identifier (e.g., USR_123456) stored only on your device.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Network Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, IP address, and preferred language to optimize rendering and gameplay stability.
  • Gameplay & Score Data: Match win/loss history, tournament scores, tokens earned via lucky spins, and leaderboard ranks.
  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID (AAID) used strictly by Google AdMob to deliver relevant advertisements.

2. Device Permissions Required

Zingarena Ludo requests only essential permissions strictly required for gameplay functionality:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively for voluntary in-game live voice chat during online multiplayer matches. Voice streams are transmitted in real-time over secure peer-to-peer/socket audio channels. We DO NOT record, transcribe, archive, or sell your voice conversations.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to establish multiplayer socket connections, synchronize tournament tables, and display AdMob ads.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We partner with trusted third-party service providers compliant with international data protection standards (including GDPR and CCPA):

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Manages secure user login without exposing credentials. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers and cookies to serve ads in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage user accounts, authenticate players, and synchronize in-game token wallets and leaderboards.
  • To match you with other real players in online multiplayer tables.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating, bot exploitation, and ensure fair competition.
  • To monitor application performance, fix crashes, and release improvements.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain account data only as long as necessary to maintain active gameplay records. Players have the absolute right to request full deletion of their account, tokens, and associated data at any time.

To request data deletion, email us at zingarenaoffi1@gmail.com with your Player ID or registered Google email. Deletion requests are fulfilled within 48-72 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

Zingarena Ludo does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such records.

7. Security of Your Information

We employ modern encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS, secure local key-value storage, and anti-tamper client verification) to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
