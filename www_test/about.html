About Zingarena Ludo

The Royal Multiplayer Board Experience

Our Mission

Zingarena Ludo was crafted by Zing Arena to bring the timeless tradition of the classic Indian royal board game (Pachisi) into a modern, fast, and competitive digital era.

Whether you are sitting with family offline on a single phone, practicing strategic moves against smart AI bots, or competing against players worldwide with real-time voice chat, Zingarena Ludo delivers smooth dice physics, authentic pawn movement, and fair competition.

Game Modes & Highlights

🤖 Play vs Computer
Enjoy high-speed offline matches powered by strategic bots without needing active internet access.
👥 Pass & Play (Local)
Gather up to 4 players on a single smartphone device with custom player names and classic token rules.
🎙️ Online Multiplayer & Voice
Create private rooms or jump into public live tables with high-fidelity live voice chat and emoji reactions.
🏆 Pro Competition & Spins
Compete on weekly global leaderboards, earn tokens through the daily lucky wheel, and climb the royal ranks.

Fair Play & Trust

We adhere strictly to fair play principles. Dice rolls are computed using cryptographically secure random distribution algorithms to guarantee that every roll of the dice is 100% genuine and untampered.
